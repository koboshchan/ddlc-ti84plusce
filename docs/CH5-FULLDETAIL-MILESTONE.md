# Full-detail motion milestone

The candidate keeps full-resolution texture geometry and the full 256-color background index space. Partially transparent foregrounds use 16 representative colors and three authored-alpha lookup tables; this is deliberately approximate, not pixel exact. DLUTQ is generated by the normal asset importer.

At 48 MHz the seven pre-empty-span-guard target samples took 170.258, 744.995, 626.984, 645.142, 876.068, 894.073 and 876.221 ms. Samples are scenes 20/26/32/32/35/35/35 at ages 0/0/0/1125/0/1125/3000 ms. Scene transitions are reset before every sample, so all seven include cold scene setup. Later age samples are not warmed repeated draws. Do not label them warm timings.

Timer scope is direct ch5_motion_draw, including palette setup, stream validation, coordinate mapping, decode, lookup preparation and compositing. It excludes UI, blit and noise. Authored animation clock is frozen independently of the real CPU timer. Normal interrupts remain enabled, with no alternate-register use.

The final production C renderer with portable assembly models passed ASAN/UBSAN over 1,261 frames. RGB5 PSNR was 35.514 dB globally and 26.978 dB minimum. Minimum exact indexed-pixel match was 29.097%; this reflects palette approximation, not a geometry mismatch. This is not a 1,261-frame target-assembly proof. Actual target assembly separately passed 1,284 reference/guard cases across all four direct kernels, counts 0 through 320, transparent and mixed source pixels, and independent doubled destination backgrounds. Full linked .text was compared to loaded RAM, not only a prefix.

Worst-error consecutive frames 1173-1180 were visually reviewed against the golden sequence. Facial details, outlines and background structure remain close, with mild wall posterization; no stale-frame ghosting or unstable shimmer was observed in that sequence.

Fresh release and debug bundles must both pass the 2,970,000-byte archive cap, per-variable caps and loader checks before publishing. The target remains approximately 100 ms; this milestone does not meet it.
